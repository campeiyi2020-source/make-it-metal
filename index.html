Who can join

The campaign is open to creators and 3D printing enthusiasts worldwide.

  • 3D designer or digital artist
  • Jewelry, product or industrial designer
  • Maker or DIY enthusiast
  • Model or toy creator
  • Craft artist or independent creator
  • Anyone curious about metal 3D printing

Your design needs to meet the model requirements below, and you need the rights or permissions to submit it. See the copyright terms for the details.

How to enter

  1. Prepare your design

    Choose a 3D model you would love to see in metal and check it against the model requirements below.

  2. Submit your entry

    Complete the SnowX Global Creator Submission Form with your contact details, your model file, and the rights and authorization information.

    Open the submission form

    The form is the main way to enter. If you write to us by email or through a community channel, we may ask you to complete the form so we can register and review your entry.

  3. Wait for the review

    Our team reviews entries for creativity, completeness and suitability for metal 3D printing. We contact selected participants using the details in their submissions.

  4. Let’s make it metal

    If your design is selected, we confirm the printing details, the estimated production timeline and the delivery arrangements with you.

  5. Receive your metal creation

    Once printing and a basic quality check are complete, we arrange delivery according to the agreed plan.

How we select

Every entry is reviewed on a rolling basis. Only designs that pass review and receive an official selection notice qualify for the free print, so submitting does not guarantee selection.

Creativity

An idea worth holding in metal.

Completeness

Campaign fit

Overall suitability for Let’s Make It Metal.

Model requirements

Supported file formats

  • F3D
  • STEP
  • IGS / IGES
  • STL
  • OBJ
  • 3MF
  • 3DM

Maximum dimensions

80 mm × 80 mm × 100 mm. The entire model must fit within this volume.

Quality and printability

  • The model has complete, clearly defined geometry.
  • The file has no major mesh errors, broken surfaces or other issues that block a proper evaluation.
  • The design is technically suitable for metal 3D printing.
  • Models that are too complex, exceed the size limit or pose significant printing challenges may not be selected.

If needed, SnowX may ask you for more information, a revised file or an editable model file to assess printability.

Rights

You must be the original creator or hold the rights to submit the design. Designs that use third-party work, trademarks, logos, characters or recognizable brand designs need permission from the rights holder first. The copyright terms explain what to declare and what you authorize.

Event rules

Limited availability

Printing spots are limited and allocated through rolling review. The campaign closes once every spot is filled.

One print per participant

You may submit several designs. Each participant can receive one free metal printing service during this campaign.

Accurate information

Give accurate contact details and the information we reasonably need to review and process your entry.

Rights and permissions

You own the rights to your design or hold the permissions to submit it and to grant the authorizations in the copyright terms.

Printing & delivery

What selected participants receive

  • One free metal 3D printing service for the selected design.
  • Standard post-processing, subject to technical evaluation and the needs of the design.
  • A basic quality inspection of the finished piece.
  • Selected content from the process and the finished piece, which may include printing progress footage and photos.

Production and delivery

  • After selection, SnowX contacts you to confirm printing details, the estimated timeline and delivery.
  • Finished pieces ship worldwide.
  • Timelines vary with model complexity, material availability, production schedules, equipment maintenance and post-processing.
  • Final dimensions, surface finish and appearance can vary with the metal printing process and post-processing, so the piece may differ slightly from your digital model.
  • SnowX may feature selected designs and finished pieces on its social media channels and tags you when it does. The copyright terms have the details.

This campaign offers a free metal 3D printing service. It does not include a free SnowPod printer or other printing equipment.

Changes & cancellation

If a selected model cannot be printed because of technical issues, SnowX makes reasonable efforts to contact you and discuss revisions or alternatives. If the issue cannot be resolved, SnowX may cancel the printing arrangement for that design.

SnowX announces significant changes through its official channels or the contact details participants provided. Participation is subject to these rules, to the copyright and authorization terms and to any privacy notice SnowX gives you.
